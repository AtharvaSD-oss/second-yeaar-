Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

InternTrack is designed with a strict client-side architecture. We believe that your job hunt, application status, salary expectations, notes, and interview details are confidential personal data that belong solely to you.

2. Data Storage and Processing

All internship records, contact details, interview logs, notes, and custom fields are stored exclusively within your browser's LocalStorage API. InternTrack does not operate a remote database, backend server, or cloud storage pipeline for your personal records. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

3. Zero Third-Party Analytics and Trackers

We do not embed third-party advertising scripts, telemetry trackers, session replay recorders, or behavioral profiling mechanisms. No marketing pixels or ad cookies are placed on your browser.

4. Data Portability and Erasure

You retain complete ownership and sovereignty over your application information:

  • Export: You can download a complete backup of all stored data at any time in standard JSON format.
  • Import: You can restore your data from any previously exported JSON backup file.
  • Deletion: You can permanently wipe all records from your browser with the Delete All Applications control in Settings, or by clearing your browser site data.

5. Browser Permissions

InternTrack may request optional permission to display desktop notifications for scheduled interview dates and urgent deadlines. This permission is handled entirely on your device by the browser Notification API and is never transmitted to an external service.

6. External Career Portals

When you click external links to apply on official company career portals (such as Google, Microsoft, Amazon, LinkedIn, or Unstop), you are navigating directly to those external websites. We do not intermediate your interactions with third-party portals, and their respective privacy policies apply.
